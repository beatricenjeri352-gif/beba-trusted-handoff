# Beba – Risk and Incident Management Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed – Not yet implemented  
**Purpose:** Define how Beba identifies, reports, investigates and manages risks and incidents involving users, deliveries, payments and the platform.

## 1. Introduction

Beba connects senders with carriers through a digital platform. Although senders make their own carrier selections, Beba must provide processes for reporting problems, recording incidents and following up on concerns.

This document establishes a framework for managing operational, financial, security and user-related risks.

Beba's role, responsibilities and potential liabilities must be clearly defined in its legal agreements and reviewed by qualified legal professionals.

## 2. Risk Management Principles

Beba's risk management approach will follow these principles:

1. Prevention: Introduce controls that reduce the likelihood of incidents.
2. Transparency: Make relevant delivery information available to authorised participants.
3. Accountability: Record important actions and decisions.
4. Reporting: Give users a clear way to report incidents.
5. Investigation: Review reported incidents using available evidence.
6. Resolution: Document outcomes and communicate appropriate updates.
7. Improvement: Use incident reports to improve platform operations.

## 3. Main Risk Categories

Beba must consider the following risk categories:

| Risk category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Identity fraud | Fake identity documents, impersonation or duplicate accounts |
| Delivery risks | Missing, damaged, delayed or incorrectly delivered parcels |
| Financial risks | Unpaid commissions, false payment confirmations or payment disputes |
| User misconduct | Harassment, threats, misleading information or misuse of contact details |
| Platform security | Unauthorised access, data exposure or account compromise |
| Operational risks | Incorrect delivery details, failed coordination or unavailable carriers |
| Business risks | Low adoption, high operating costs or insufficient carrier participation |

## 4. Missing or Lost Parcels

A parcel may be reported missing when:
- The carrier does not deliver the parcel.
- The receiver does not receive the parcel.
- The delivery status indicates completion but the receiver disputes receipt.
- The carrier cannot account for the parcel.

### Response process

1. Sender or receiver reports the missing parcel.
2. Beba creates an incident record linked to the delivery.
3. The delivery status is reviewed.
4. Beba contacts the relevant parties for clarification.
5. Available evidence is collected.
6. The incident is investigated.
7. The outcome and follow-up actions are recorded.

Evidence may include:
- Collection confirmation.
- Delivery confirmation.
- Relevant timestamps.
- Carrier and receiver details.
- Parcel photographs, where available.
- Relevant communication records.

Beba should not automatically treat a carrier's delivery update as proof that the receiver received the parcel.

## 5. Damaged Parcels

When a parcel is reported damaged:

1. The sender or receiver submits an incident report.
2. The user provides a description of the damage.
3. Photographs or other supporting evidence may be submitted.
4. Beba records the incident and contacts the relevant parties.
5. The investigation and outcome are documented.

Beba must distinguish between damage reported before collection and damage reported after delivery.

The platform should provide a clear reporting process without promising compensation or accepting liability unless an applicable policy or legal obligation requires it.

## 6. Failed Deliveries

A delivery may fail because:
- The receiver is unavailable.
- The destination is incorrect.
- The carrier cannot reach the destination.
- The sender provides incorrect information.
- The carrier becomes unavailable.
- The delivery arrangement cannot be completed.

The system should record:
- Reason for failure.
- Person reporting the failure.
- Date and time.
- Supporting information.
- Any agreed next steps.

Possible outcomes include rescheduling, returning the parcel, cancellation or escalation.

Any additional charges or commission implications must follow Beba's approved pricing and payment policy.

## 7. Receiver Does Not Confirm Receipt

A receiver may fail to confirm receipt even when a carrier reports successful delivery.

In this situation:

1. The delivery must remain awaiting confirmation or review.
2. Beba may send a reminder.
3. The sender or carrier may contact the receiver.
4. Beba may investigate if confirmation remains unresolved.
5. The incident must be recorded if a dispute is raised.

Silence must not automatically be treated as proof of successful delivery.

Where an unresolved delivery requires restrictions, Beba may temporarily prevent the relevant account from accepting new deliveries, subject to its policies.

Users must retain access to support, tracking information and incident reporting.

## 8. Identity Fraud and Impersonation

Potential identity-related incidents include:
- Someone registering using another person's identity document.
- A person accepting a delivery using another carrier's account.
- Duplicate identity information.
- Suspicious account changes.
- Attempts to bypass verification.

### Response process

1. Flag the suspicious account or activity.
2. Restrict affected account functions where justified.
3. Preserve relevant records.
4. Refer the matter for authorised human review.
5. Request additional verification where appropriate.
6. Record the investigation outcome.
7. Restore or restrict account access based on the outcome.

Only authorised personnel should access identity verification information.

## 9. Payment and Commission Incidents

Potential financial incidents include:
- Carrier fails to pay Beba's commission.
- Payment is made but not reflected in the account.
- Duplicate payment records.
- False payment screenshots.
- Payment reference mismatches.
- Incorrect commission calculations.

### Response process

1. Record the affected delivery and transaction.
2. Check the payment reference and amount.
3. Verify payment through the payment provider or reconciliation process.
4. Investigate any discrepancy.
5. Correct the payment record through an authorised process.
6. Record the final outcome.

A carrier should not be marked as having paid a commission based solely on an unverified screenshot.

## 10. User Misconduct and Safety Incidents

Beba should provide a reporting channel for:
- Threatening behaviour.
- Harassment.
- Abusive communication.
- Misuse of personal contact information.
- Fraudulent activity.
- Attempts to obtain unauthorised access to parcels or accounts.

Reports should be reviewed promptly according to their seriousness.

Where there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, users should be directed to contact the appropriate emergency services. Beba's reporting process is not a replacement for emergency assistance.

## 11. Security and Data Breach Incidents

Security incidents may include:
- Unauthorised access to accounts.
- Exposure of identity documents.
- Compromised administrator credentials.
- Database access by unauthorised parties.
- Loss or disclosure of personal information.

Response steps:

1. Report and record the incident.
2. Contain the affected system or account.
3. Preserve relevant technical evidence.
4. Assess the information and users affected.
5. Investigate the cause.
6. Take corrective action.
7. Notify affected parties and authorities where legally required.
8. Record lessons and preventive measures.

Security incidents must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

## 12. Incident Reporting System

Beba should provide an incident-reporting function accessible to authorised users.

A report should capture:
- Incident reference.
- Related delivery reference.
- Reporting user.
- Incident category.
- Description.
- Date and time reported.
- Supporting evidence.
- Current status.
- Assigned reviewer.
- Investigation notes.
- Resolution and closure date.

### Suggested incident statuses

- Reported.
- Under review.
- Awaiting information.
- Investigation in progress.
- Resolved.
- Closed.

## 13. Escalation and Investigation

Incidents should be assigned according to their seriousness.

| Priority | Example | Expected response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate safety threat or major data breach | Urgent escalation and containment |
| High | Missing parcel, suspected fraud or disputed delivery | Prompt investigation |
| Medium | Damaged parcel or unresolved delivery | Standard investigation |
| Low | Minor coordination issue or general complaint | Normal support process |

Specific response deadlines must be established before the pilot.

Only authorised Beba personnel should close investigations and make administrative decisions.

## 14. Risk Prevention Measures

Beba should implement:
- Manual carrier identity verification.
- Approved carrier profiles.
- Delivery milestone records.
- Collection and receiver confirmation processes.
- Secure account authentication.
- Protected delivery evidence.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Audit logs.
- Incident reporting.
- Clear user agreements and privacy notices.

## 15. Risk Register

Beba should maintain a separate risk register containing:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk ID | Unique risk reference |
| Risk description | What could go wrong |
| Likelihood | Estimated chance of occurrence |
| Impact | Potential consequences |
| Risk level | Overall risk classification |
| Preventive controls | Measures to reduce risk |
| Response plan | Actions if the risk occurs |
| Owner | Person responsible for monitoring |
| Status | Open, monitoring or resolved |

## 16. Outstanding Decisions

Before the pilot, Beba must finalise:
- Incident response timeframes.
- Internal incident ownership.
- Escalation contacts.
- Account restriction and appeal procedures.
- Missing and damaged parcel policies.
- Dispute resolution procedures.
- Evidence retention periods.
- Applicable legal responsibilities and insurance considerations.
