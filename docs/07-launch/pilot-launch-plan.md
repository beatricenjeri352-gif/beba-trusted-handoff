# Beba – Pilot Launch Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed – Planning stage  
**Purpose:** Define how Beba will introduce its initial working product to a controlled group of users, evaluate its performance and prepare for wider expansion.

## 1. Introduction

The purpose of the Beba pilot is to test the platform in a real operating environment before making it available to a wider market.

The pilot will help Beba understand:
- Whether senders find the platform useful.
- Whether carriers are willing to register and participate.
- Whether users understand the delivery process.
- Whether the pricing model is commercially viable.
- Whether the platform can support safe and reliable delivery coordination.
- What needs to improve before expansion.

The pilot is not intended to prove that every possible feature is complete. It is intended to validate the core product and operating model.

## 2. Pilot Objectives

The pilot will aim to:

1. Validate the sender-first delivery process.
2. Test carrier registration and manual approval.
3. Evaluate carrier discovery and direct contact.
4. Test delivery creation and assignment.
5. Evaluate delivery tracking and receiver confirmation.
6. Test pricing and commission collection.
7. Identify operational risks.
8. Collect feedback from real users.
9. Establish whether Beba is ready for a wider launch.

## 3. Initial Target Market

Beba is initially intended for the Kenyan market.

The first pilot location must be selected before recruitment begins.

The initial pilot should focus on a limited geographical area where Beba can monitor deliveries and provide practical support.

Potential user groups include:

### Senders
- Individuals sending personal parcels.
- Small businesses.
- Online sellers.
- Businesses requiring regular deliveries.

### Carriers
- Independent delivery providers.
- Approved individual carriers.
- Other eligible transport providers.

### Receivers
- Individuals receiving parcels.
- Business recipients.
- Designated collection contacts.

The exact pilot location and participant numbers remain to be confirmed.

## 4. Pilot Scope

The pilot should focus on the core MVP workflow.

### Included features

- User registration and authentication.
- Sender and carrier profiles.
- Manual carrier identity verification.
- Carrier approval.
- Delivery request creation.
- Pricing calculation.
- Approved carrier directory.
- Carrier profile viewing.
- Sender-to-carrier contact.
- Sender carrier selection.
- Carrier acceptance or rejection.
- Delivery milestone updates.
- Receiver confirmation.
- Commission calculation and recording.
- Incident reporting.
- Basic administrative controls.

### Excluded or deferred features

Unless specifically approved for the pilot, the following should remain outside the initial scope:

- Nationwide expansion.
- Automated carrier assignment.
- Complex live GPS tracking.
- Advanced route optimisation.
- Automated facial recognition developed by Beba.
- Advanced analytics dashboards.
- Complex business integrations.
- Features not required to test the core delivery process.

## 5. Pilot Preparation

Before onboarding users, Beba must complete:

- MVP development.
- Functional and integration testing.
- Security and privacy checks.
- User terms and privacy documentation.
- Carrier verification procedures.
- Pricing and commission configuration.
- Payment reconciliation process.
- Incident reporting procedures.
- Support and escalation arrangements.
- Backup and recovery checks.

The pilot must not begin with real identity documents or financial transactions until the necessary safeguards and legal requirements have been addressed.

## 6. Carrier Recruitment and Onboarding

Carriers must be recruited and approved before they can participate.

The onboarding process should include:

1. Carrier registration.
2. Submission of required identity information through an approved secure process.
3. Manual identity review.
4. Verification decision.
5. Carrier profile completion.
6. Explanation of Beba's commission model.
7. Explanation of delivery and confirmation procedures.
8. Agreement to applicable platform terms.
9. Activation of the approved carrier account.

Carriers should understand that Beba provides the platform for discovery and coordination. Senders choose which carriers to contact and select.

## 7. Sender Onboarding

Senders should be introduced to Beba through a simple onboarding process.

They should understand how to:

1. Create an account.
2. Create a delivery request.
3. Review the calculated delivery price.
4. Browse approved carriers.
5. Contact carriers directly or through the supported contact method.
6. Select a carrier.
7. Monitor delivery progress.
8. Confirm receipt or report an issue.

Users should receive clear instructions on how Beba works and what responsibilities remain with the sender and carrier.

## 8. Pilot Delivery Workflow

The pilot will follow this agreed sequence:

1. Sender creates a delivery request.
2. Beba calculates and displays the price.
3. Sender reviews available approved carriers.
4. Sender contacts the preferred carrier.
5. Sender and carrier agree on the delivery arrangement.
6. Sender selects the carrier.
7. Carrier accepts or declines the assignment.
8. Carrier collects the parcel.
9. Carrier updates delivery progress.
10. Receiver confirms receipt.
11. Beba records completion.
12. Carrier's commission is calculated and settled according to the agreed process.

This process must remain consistent with the approved product requirements.

## 9. Pilot Operations and Support

Beba should assign responsibility for:
- Carrier onboarding.
- User support.
- Identity verification.
- Delivery monitoring.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Incident reporting.
- Dispute investigation.
- Platform maintenance.

A support channel should be available to participants during the pilot.

Users should know how to report problems and what to do in an urgent safety situation.

## 10. Pilot Duration and Phases

The pilot duration will be decided after the initial location, participant group and operational capacity have been established.

Proposed phases:

### Phase 1: Preparation
- Complete the MVP.
- Complete testing.
- Prepare policies and procedures.
- Recruit initial carriers.

### Phase 2: Controlled onboarding
- Introduce a limited number of senders and carriers.
- Explain the platform.
- Collect onboarding feedback.

### Phase 3: Live pilot
- Allow approved users to create and complete real delivery requests.
- Monitor delivery progress.
- Record issues and feedback.
- Review payment and commission processes.

### Phase 4: Evaluation
- Analyse pilot results.
- Identify product gaps.
- Review user satisfaction.
- Assess operational and financial performance.
- Decide whether to improve, extend or expand the pilot.

## 11. Success Metrics

Beba should measure the following indicators.

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Registered senders | Adoption among potential customers |
| Approved carriers | Available carrier participation |
| Delivery requests created | Platform usage |
| Carrier selection rate | Whether senders find suitable carriers |
| Carrier acceptance rate | Carrier participation |
| Completed deliveries | Successful delivery outcomes |
| Failed deliveries | Operational reliability |
| Receiver confirmation rate | Completion verification |
| Average time to complete delivery | Delivery efficiency |
| Disputes and incidents | Operational risk |
| Commission collection rate | Revenue collection effectiveness |
| Repeat sender usage | Customer retention |
| User satisfaction | Overall experience |

Targets must be established before the pilot starts. Results should be interpreted against the size and nature of the pilot rather than treated as universal benchmarks.

## 12. User Feedback Collection

Feedback should be collected from senders, carriers and receivers.

Suggested questions:

### Senders
- Was it easy to create a delivery request?
- Were carrier profiles useful?
- Did you feel informed when selecting a carrier?
- Was the pricing clear?
- Would you use Beba again?

### Carriers
- Was registration straightforward?
- Were delivery requests clear?
- Was the commission process understandable?
- Were the delivery updates easy to use?
- What would improve your experience?

### Receivers
- Was it easy to understand the delivery information?
- Was confirming receipt straightforward?
- Did you know how to report a problem?

Feedback may be collected through interviews, short surveys and support records.

## 13. Pilot Risks

The pilot must monitor:

- Insufficient carrier participation.
- Low sender adoption.
- Unclear pricing.
- Users bypassing Beba's commission model.
- Delivery failures.
- Identity fraud.
- Payment reconciliation problems.
- Privacy and security incidents.
- Insufficient operational support.

Relevant risks must be managed through the Risk and Incident Management Plan.

## 14. Pilot Exit Criteria

The pilot should only progress to wider expansion when:

1. Core delivery workflows operate reliably.
2. Carriers can be verified and approved.
3. Senders can successfully create and manage delivery requests.
4. Carrier selection and acceptance work correctly.
5. Delivery completion and receiver confirmation are recorded reliably.
6. Pricing and commission calculations are accurate.
7. Payment reconciliation is operational.
8. Incident reporting and investigation processes work.
9. No unresolved critical security issues remain.
10. User feedback supports further development.
11. Operational and financial feasibility has been assessed.

## 15. Post-Pilot Improvements

After evaluation, Beba should produce a pilot findings report containing:

- Number of participating users.
- Number of approved carriers.
- Number of delivery requests.
- Number of completed deliveries.
- Failed and disputed delivery cases.
- Commission collected.
- User feedback.
- Technical issues.
- Operational challenges.
- Recommended improvements.
- Expansion recommendation.

The next development cycle should be based on actual pilot findings.

## 16. Outstanding Decisions

Before launch, Beba must confirm:

- Initial pilot town or geographical area.
- Pilot duration.
- Target number of senders.
- Target number of carriers.
- Recruitment approach.
- Support responsibilities.
- Commission reconciliation process.
- Pilot budget.
- Success targets.
- Launch approval responsibilities.
