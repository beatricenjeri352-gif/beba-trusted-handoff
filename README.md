# Beba – Delivery Mtaani

**A trusted goods hand-off and delivery coordination platform.**

Beba aims to connect businesses, senders, carriers and recipients, making goods transportation easier, safer and more transparent.

## The Problem

Moving goods around local markets and towns can be challenging when people lack reliable delivery connections and clear hand-off processes.

## Our Approach

Beba explores a trusted delivery coordination system that helps users:

- Request goods transportation.
- Connect with available carriers.
- Confirm parcel collection and delivery.
- Track hand-offs and build trust through delivery evidence.

## Project Status

Early-stage prototype. Currently under development.
